# CREOVA Competitive Creative Research

Date: 2026-11-10

## Benchmarks reviewed

### Narrative XPR

Observed strengths:

- Project proof enters quickly and dominates the page.
- Compact editorial headings let imagery carry emotional weight.
- Linework, asymmetric placements, and selective accents build rhythm without turning every item into a card.

Risks to reject:

- Large inactive gaps can read as loading failures.
- Neon accents would conflict with CREOVA’s grounded cultural palette.
- Visual novelty must not replace clear services and next steps.

### OneMethod

Observed strengths:

- Work fills the viewport and operates as the main brand argument.
- Section moods change deliberately through scale, crop, and negative space.
- Large type is reserved for a few brand moments instead of every page introduction.

Risks to reject:

- Excessive black space can feel empty rather than cinematic.
- Sparse semantics and missing heading structure are not acceptable models.
- CREOVA should not adopt agency swagger that exceeds its published evidence.

## Comparative matrix

| Dimension | CREOVA current | Narrative XPR | OneMethod | CREOVA response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First-screen anchor | Huge headline over authentic photo | Immediate project-led composition | Dark, image-forward brand moment | Keep authentic photography; reduce title to reveal people and place. |
| Work proof | Rich library but can paint late | Dense and immediate | Large, immersive work panels | Render work reliably and vary image scale. |
| Typography | Display scale repeated across routes | Compact editorial hierarchy | Selective dramatic scale | Use restrained role-based type tokens. |
| Layout rhythm | Frequent three-card grids | Lines, columns, asymmetric modules | Viewport-scale shifts | Alternate full-bleed, split editorial, index rows, and selective cards. |
| Colour | Distinct burgundy/cream/gold | Mostly neutral with vivid accent | Mostly black/white | Preserve CREOVA palette; use gold as a signal, not decoration. |
| Claims | Conservative and evidence-aware | Agency marketing language | Bold studio positioning | Keep verified/public/hypothesis boundaries explicit. |

## Adopt, reject, reinterpret

### Adopt

- Immediate project proof.
- Fewer but stronger typographic moments.
- Varied image crops and editorial pacing.
- Clear section jobs and scannable headlines.

### Reject

- Generic stock imagery.
- Neon or trend-driven colours unrelated to CREOVA.
- Empty cinematic space without narrative purpose.
- Unverified outcome metrics or implied client endorsements.

### Reinterpret for CREOVA

- Replace agency spectacle with cultural intimacy: gestures, faces, rooms, and exchanges.
- Use burgundy fields as quiet editorial pauses between photographic sequences.
- Pair every visual moment with context about people, place, purpose, and permitted use.
- Treat bilingual content as an equal design state, not a translation afterthought.
